# Committee UI V4

## Main change
The Committee Term is now a single committee-wide concept.

- Removed the four Committee KPI cards at the top.
- Added one full-width Committee Term Progress area in their place.
- The top term area shows:
  - committee term dates
  - overall progress
  - days remaining
  - inline counts for positions, assigned, vacant, available, and on leave

## Role display
Individual roles are no longer rendered as cards/boxes.

They are displayed as a flat interactive roster with:
- role + group
- assigned member
- contact/email
- status
- actions
- expandable detail rows

Rows are separated by simple dividers rather than individual containers.

## Term dates
Assignments use the same committee-wide term dates. The modal automatically fills the common committee term.


## Fixed Committee Term
- Start: 01 January 2026
- End: 31 December 2027
- All assignments use this same committee-wide term.
